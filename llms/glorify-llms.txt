# Glorify

> Glorify is a Christian devotional and worship mobile application for building a daily faith and wellbeing practice — daily devotionals, Bible passages, guided meditations, prayer, sleep stories, worship music, and audio courses, plus a community and the Glorify Ring wearable. Reported 20M+ downloads and a 4.9-star average over 1M+ reviews. Backed by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z). Glorify is a consumer application and does not currently publish a public developer API, SDK, or developer portal.
